Acoustic drum samples: Salamander Drumkit by Alexander Holm
Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderDrumkit  (author page: https://rytmenpinne.wordpress.com/sounds-and-such/salamander-drumkit/)

The author later declared the kit public domain ("As of 4.3.2022, this is now public domain!") on the page above.

These files are adapted from the originals: leading silence trimmed, shortened with fade-out, mixed to mono,
and encoded as 96 kbps MP3. To be safe under either statement, these adapted samples are offered under
CC BY-SA 3.0 with attribution to Alexander Holm. The rest of this app's code is unaffected (the share-alike
condition applies to the samples themselves).

2026-11 tom refresh: high and floor toms re-processed from the same Salamander OH
WAVs with soft limiting to -1 dBFS (the source hi-tom peaks were above digital
full scale and clipped). Mid-tom files (tomMid_*) are new: Salamander has no mid
tom, so they are formant-preserved rubberband pitch-shifts of the hi-tom samples
down to the kit's mid pitch (~106 Hz), then trimmed/faded/mono/MP3 the same way.
Previously the mid voice reused the floor-tom files at playback rate 1.22, which
sounded thin and distorted.
