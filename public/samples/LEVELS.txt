Gain staging for the three natural-pitch kits. Measured on the encoded MP3s with ffmpeg astats.
Not an audition. No file was peak-normalised or limited.

Voice gain is set so a full-velocity hit of that voice reaches about the same level, at the
compressor input, as the Salamander acoustic kit: kick/snare/toms match the acoustic kick,
hats/ride/bell/shaker match the acoustic closed hat, crashes match acoustic crash 1 after the
existing runtime crash curve (0.708 * velocity^1.5). The Salamander open-hat -6 dB and crash 2
-3 dB trims were not copied.

If a soft crash (velocity 0.5, through that same curve) landed more than 6 dB under the acoustic
soft crash, crash gains were raised until it was within 6 dB, and not so far that a full crash
exceeded the acoustic full crash by more than 3 dB.
