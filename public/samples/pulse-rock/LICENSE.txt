What changed from the 2023 repack:
- Kept the center articulations Pulse songs use: 26" kick, 14" snare, sidestick, 14" rack tom,
  16" floor tom, closed hat, pedal hat, semi-open hat (used for the open-hat voice), ride bow,
  ride bell, 20" crash, 17" crash, 10" splash (splash variant only), china, cowbell, maraca.
- The mid tom is the same 14" rack tom files, not a new drum and not a pitch shift.
  Edge hits were not used as a fake round robin or a fake third tom.
- Upstream SFZ exclusive groups that choke a cymbal when the same cymbal repeats were not imported.
  Choke samples were not included.
- Encoded to VBR MP3 (LAME -q:a 2) for delivery. No peak normalisation, no limiter, no fade,
  no resampling, no pitch shift. Recorded dynamics and tails are otherwise intact.
- This adaptation was not auditioned by ear.
